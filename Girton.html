Girton Holiday Club

Where play meets sports and every child is the MVP!

Where glitter meets Football

With over 20 years of experience in childcare and sports coaching, we’re proud to offer the best of both worlds creative play and exciting sports to keep children active, engaged, and smiling all day.

Kids will explore child-led play zones with support from our staff — from blocks and crafts to role play and beyond. They’ll also have the chance to join in sports games like dodgeball, football, and so much more. A perfect mix of creativity and activity, keeping children engaged all day!

  • ⚽ Organised sports
  • 🎨 Craft tables, Lego building, chill out zones
  • 🏃‍♀️ Obstacle courses and relay fun
  • 🗣️ Group games that build confidence & friendships
